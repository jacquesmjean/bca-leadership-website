BCA Vision 2030
2030BCA's vision, in its own words

It is 2030. BCA is the most visible catalyst for exponential growth in organizations across Africa, whether public, private, or non-profit. We are the leadership enhancement partner that leaders of organizations turn to first.

BCA Leadership is a Pan-African leadership enhancement organization. It was created to help leaders of organizations raise their leadership capacity and performance, which in turn benefits their organizations, their stakeholders, and their communities.

01

Most Visible Catalyst

The clearest driver of growth for organizations across Africa, and the name leaders trust when they want to get better.

02

Every Sector

Public, private, and non-profit organizations alike. Leadership matters in all of them.

03

Exponential Growth

Not incremental gains, but step-change performance for the organizations BCA serves.

04

Preferred Partner

The leadership enhancement partner of choice for leaders across the continent.

Our Values

What we stand on.

Three commitments shape how BCA plans, works, and shows up for the leaders and communities we serve.

i.

Integrity

We believe that integrity is the bedrock of everything we plan or do, that trust is essential to unity and purpose, and that honesty and transparency enable accountability. We honor our commitments.

ii.

Growth

We believe that growth is essential, continual, and permeates every aspect of our individual and corporate character and beliefs, knowledge, and capabilities.

iii.

Service

We believe that service is both a noble calling and a winning strategy, leading us to prioritize the needs of our clients, agents, communities, nations, and continent above our own and to strive to meet those needs with excellence.

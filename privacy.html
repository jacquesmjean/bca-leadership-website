Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal data BCA Leadership collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the rights you have over it.

This document is available in French, Spanish and Portuguese for convenience. In the event of any discrepancy, the English version governs.

Last updated: 12 July 2026
This page is a working draft prepared for BCA Leadership. It should be confirmed by qualified Mauritian legal counsel, and reviewed against the Mauritius Data Protection Act 2017 and the EU GDPR where members are in Europe, before final publication.

1. Who we are

BCA Leadership, care of FFG (Mauritius) Ltd, Grand Baie, Mauritius, is the controller of the personal data described here. We decide why and how your data is used. You can reach us at admin@bcaleadership.com.

2. Data we collect

We collect only what we need to run the membership network and provide coaching.

WhatWhen
Name, email, company, countryWhen you apply for membership or coaching, or contact us.
Your agreement record: the tier or package chosen, the version of the agreement, and the date and time you signedWhen you sign a membership or coaching agreement on the site.
Payment detailsHandled directly by Stripe when you are invoiced. We receive confirmation of payment, not your full card number.
Basic technical data, such as browser type and approximate location from your IP addressAutomatically, to keep the site secure and working.

3. How we use it

  • to set up and manage your membership or coaching;
  • to record the agreement you signed and to raise your invoice;
  • to contact you about your membership, events, and the programs you join;
  • to keep the site secure and to improve how it works;
  • to meet our legal and accounting obligations.

4. Our legal basis

Where the law requires a legal basis, we rely on: performing the agreement you signed with us, our legitimate interest in running and improving the network, your consent for optional communications, and compliance with legal duties such as record-keeping.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with the providers that help us operate, and only as needed:

  • Stripe processes payments and invoicing.
  • Supabase hosts the secure database where your details and agreement record are stored.
  • Google Fonts serves the site's typography, which may involve your browser requesting font files from Google.
  • YouTube (Google) serves the testimonial videos embedded on the site. When you play a video, YouTube may set its own cookies.
  • TechFides builds and maintains the site on our behalf, under confidentiality.

We may also disclose data if the law requires it, or to protect our rights and the safety of our members.

6. Cookies and tracking

The site uses only the cookies it needs to function and stay secure. We do not use advertising cookies. Some third-party features, such as embedded YouTube videos, may set their own cookies when you interact with them. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

7. International transfers

BCA serves members across Africa and beyond, and our providers operate in different countries. This means your data may be processed outside your own country, including in regions with different data protection laws. Where we transfer data internationally, we take steps to protect it consistent with applicable law.

8. How long we keep it

We keep your membership and agreement records for as long as you are a member, and afterward for the period we need to meet legal, tax, and accounting requirements. When we no longer need your data, we delete or anonymize it.

9. How we protect it

Your data is stored in a secured database with access controls, so that only authorized systems and people can reach it. Payment details are handled by Stripe and are not stored on our own servers. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and to respond quickly if a problem arises.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the data we hold about you;
  • correct data that is wrong or out of date;
  • ask us to delete your data, where the law allows;
  • object to or restrict certain uses of your data;
  • withdraw consent for optional communications at any time;
  • ask for your data in a portable format.

To exercise any of these, email admin@bcaleadership.com. If you are in the European Union or another region with a data protection authority, you also have the right to complain to that authority.

11. Children

Our services are for professionals aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has given us data, please contact us and we will remove it.

12. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed. We encourage you to review it periodically.

13. Contact us

For any question about your data or this policy, email admin@bcaleadership.com, or write to BCA Leadership, BCA Leadership, Suite 113, First Floor, Grand Baie Business Park Phase 1, Grand Baie, Mauritius.

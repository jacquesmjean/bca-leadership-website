How Membership Works

The monthly rhythm of a sharper leader.

Every BCA cohort meeting runs the same disciplined rhythm, refined across nine years and 3,000+ leaders.

01

Knowledge Acquisition

A guest speaker opens each meeting with a talk on a relevant topic, followed by facilitated knowledge-sharing among members.

02

Peer Problem-Solving

Half of every meeting is devoted to your problems, professional or personal. Every problem you face has been solved by another member before.

03

Individual Coaching

A private, one-hour session each month with your chosen leadership coach. Confidential, certified, accountable.

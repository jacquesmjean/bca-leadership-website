A note on the other languages

The site runs in English, French, Spanish and Portuguese. A client reading in French sees the coaching agreement in French, with this line beneath it:

"Ce contrat vous est présenté en français à titre indicatif. La version anglaise est celle que vous signez et fait foi en cas de divergence."

In other words, the English text is the version being signed and governs in any dispute. The same applies to the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy. Your counsel only needs to approve the English.

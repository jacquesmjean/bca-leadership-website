Africa’s Most Experienced Leaders Are Disappearing at the Height of Their Wisdom. A New Initiative Exists to Change That.

By BCA Leadership · May 21, 2026

Press Release: 21st May, 2026

BCA Leadership and Coach Africa announce Legacies of Africa, a structured executive transition programme designed for the continent’s senior leaders entering a new season of contribution.

There is a moment that arrives without announcement. A final board meeting. A handover ceremony. A last signature on a document of consequence. And then, for thousands of Africa’s most accomplished leaders every year, a silence that no one prepared them for.

This silence is not personal. It is structural. And its consequences extend well beyond the individuals who experience it.

Africa’s institutions were built by a generation of leaders who navigated independence movements, founded central banks, stabilized currencies, opened universities, and carried their nations through transitions that no governance manual had charted. That experience does not become irrelevant when transition hits. It becomes, at that precise moment, the most transferable asset on the continent. The question that has never been adequately answered is how to transfer it.

What disappears when a senior leader leaves formal office without a structured pathway forward is not just one career, it is decades of institutional memory, a cultivated network of trust, a refined capacity for judgment under pressure, and a set of relationships that took a lifetime to build.

When that disappears, it does not simply represent a personal loss. It represents a governance continuity failure. It represents a gap in the mentorship pipeline that the next generation of African leaders will navigate without the guidance it requires. It represents a fracture in the intergenerational chain through which wisdom is supposed to move.

This is the continental leadership challenge that BCA Leadership and Coach Africa have come together to address. The partnership is deliberate. BCA Leadership brings a pan-African presence spanning more than thirty countries, deep credibility at the CEO and board level, and a track record of convening the continent’s most consequential leaders in peer learning environments where frank exchange is possible and institutional authority is respected.

Coach Africa brings twenty years of leadership development, coaching excellence rooted in African context, accredited methodologies, and a specific mastery in the work of transition: the structured, disciplined process of helping leaders move not away from their former roles, but forward into their full purpose.

These are not complementary organisations that happened to find common ground. They are two institutions that recognized the same gap from different vantage points and concluded that only a collaboration of this caliber could address it with the seriousness it deserves. The result is Legacies of Africa.

The programme is a six-month structured transition pathway designed for leaders who have completed a significant chapter of formal institutional authority and who are ready to approach what comes next with the same rigor and intentionality they brought to the roles they just held.

Legacies of Africa is not a ceremonial acknowledgment of service. It is not a retirement offering.

It is a structured engagement with a complex transition that most leaders navigate in isolation, without framework, without peers, and without the kind of honest counsel that their former positions rarely permitted. The programme is designed for leaders who understand that the question before them is not what to do with their time. It is how to convert years of hard-won judgment into something the continent can inherit.

Participants will leave with a defined legacy blueprint, a credible platform positioned for governance, advisory, and mentorship contribution, and a twelve-month plan for implementation grounded in accountability and peer support. The cohort itself is a deliberate part of the design.

Beyond audiences, these senior leaders deserve circles of peers who engage with their wisdom, safeguard their legacies, and ensure their knowledge endures for future generations.

Legacies of Africa exists to ensure that Africa’s most experienced leaders do not disappear at the height of their wisdom. The continent cannot afford that disappearance. The institutions that still need these leaders as advisors and governors cannot afford it. The next generation of leadership, which requires the kind of mentorship that can only come from lived experience at the highest levels, cannot afford it.

Private briefings and institutional conversations are now being scheduled.

Enquiries may be directed to BCA Leadership and Coach Africa through established institutional channels.

About BCA Leadership

BCA Leadership is a pan-African leadership enhancement institution operating across more than thirty countries of Africa with a network of over 40 world-class coaches, trainers and consultants. We specialize in leadership training, executive coaching, organizational development consulting, and event management for Directors and executive teams in all five regions of Africa.

About Coach Africa

Coach Africa is an award-winning pan-African leadership development and accredited coach training institution founded in Uganda in 2006. Over nearly two decades, it has developed leaders across public, private, and development sectors throughout the continent, and earned recognition as Coaching and Consulting Organisation of the Year at the Africa Coaching and Consulting Awards. Its methodologies are African in orientation and rigorous in execution.

Questions leaders ask us.

How is BCA different from global executive networks?

Global programs are excellent in their home markets. BCA was built for Africa from day one: 40+ certified coaches and consultants living and working across the continent, peer rooms filled with leaders in your markets, and pricing set for African market realities.

Who will my coach be?

You choose one of the two cohort facilitators in your cohort as your personal leadership coach. These are certified professionals whose careers span Honeywell, Shell, Kenya Airways, the World Bank, and boards across Africa.

What happens in a cohort meeting?

A guest speaker opens with a relevant topic; members discuss. Then half the meeting goes to peer problem-solving: you bring the problem, your peers bring solutions. Every problem you face has been solved by another member before.

Is everything confidential?

Absolutely. Cohorts run under strict confidentiality, and your coaching relationship is private. What is said in the room stays in the room.

What results do members see?

63% of member CEOs experience revenue growth above 10%. Beyond the numbers: sharper decisions, stronger boards, and a peer network that has your back across the continent.

"The peer network has my back across the continent. That's worth more than the fee." · Maryam Darwich

Frequently asked questions.

What's the main focus of this community?

BCAOnline is the always-on extension of the BCA network: a private space where leaders and coaches across Africa connect between meetings, sharpen their thinking, share knowledge, and find partners and opportunities. It's built for leaders who never stop learning.

How much does it cost to join?

BCAOnline access is included with BCA membership. You can also join the community directly. See current plans and pricing on the access page. View plans →

Can I invite other leaders in my network?

Yes. The network grows by introduction. Once you're inside, you can invite leaders and coaches in your circle, and use Needs & Leads to make warm, cross-border connections.

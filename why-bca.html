Five reasons leaders choose BCA.

A coaching directory can give you a name. BCA gives you a room of people who have already solved the problem on your desk.

01

Operators, not just advisors

Our coaches and consultants have sat in your chair. They have led banks, built companies, and chaired boards across the continent. You get counsel from people who have carried the weight, not theory from the sidelines.

Peer rooms of African CEOs

You stop solving alone. Confidential monthly cohorts put you in a room with leaders facing the same decisions, in the same markets. What is shared in a BCA room stays in the room.

From Lagos to Nairobi, Casablanca to Cape Town, and into the diaspora. Certified coaches and senior operators in finance, law, health, telecoms, and more, working in English, French, Arabic, and beyond.

Priced for African market realities

World-class development should not require a Western budget. Our membership and coaching are set for the leaders and organizations we serve, so more of the continent can reach the room.

Coaching, peer learning, consulting, project management, and business matching, delivered by the same bench. One relationship that grows with you, instead of five vendors who do not talk to each other.

Outcomes the board can measure

Sharper decisions, stronger teams, and revenue that moves. Most of our member CEOs report double-digit revenue growth. This is development you can point to, not a certificate on the wall.
